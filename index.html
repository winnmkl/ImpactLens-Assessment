Information Classification & Disposal

CIA dimensions are rated 1–5 (ISO 27005 / NIST SP 800-60). The classification label is driven by the highest of C, I, or A — not the sum. Example: C=2, I=2, A=2 → max 2 → Internal Use (sum 6 is informational only).

ClassificationMax C/I/ATypical sum rangeDescriptionHard copy disposalSoft copy disposal
Public13–5May be distributed externally without material harm. No watermark required.Standard waste paper; strip-cut if in doubt.Delete file; physical media: destroy if sensitive.
Internal Use24–8For employees and authorized contractors only. Watermark: "CONTROLLED COPY".Strip-cut shredder or confidential waste bin.Secure delete; physical media: certified destruction.
Confidential36–11Requires protection; limited circulation on need-to-know. Watermark: "CONFIDENTIAL".Strip-cut shredder or confidential waste bin.Overwrite + secure delete; approved destruction for media.
Restricted4–59–15Highest protection; strict logging of access and distribution. Watermark: "RESTRICTED".Cross-cut shredder + destruction record.Crypto-shred / overwrite + audit trail; approved destruction.
Threat & weakness bands (1–5 per row)

Each risk scenario supports multiple threat and weakness entries. The engine uses the worst (highest) band per type, centered on 3 (±2 adjusts inherent probability / severity before qualitative ratings and escalations).

BandThreat hostility (→ probability)Weakness / exposure (→ severity)
1Rare / no credible adversaryHardened / minimal gaps
2Unlikely targeted interestLow residual exposure
3Moderate (baseline)Moderate weaknesses
4High — capable adversaryMaterial control gaps
5Critical — targeted / insider abuseCritical exposure
